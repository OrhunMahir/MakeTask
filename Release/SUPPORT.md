# MakeTask Support

MakeTask is a local macOS menu-bar app for independent desktop task notes.

## Contact

[Open a support issue on GitHub](https://github.com/OrhunMahir/MakeTask/issues). Include your MakeTask version from Settings → About, macOS version, what you expected, and the steps that caused the problem. GitHub issues are public: do not attach private tasks or full backups.

## Where did the app go?

MakeTask lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen and does not keep a Dock icon. Click its checklist icon to create or reveal lists. The welcome window appears on a new, empty installation; reopen it with “Welcome to MakeTask…” in the menu.

## A note disappeared

Click the menu-bar icon and choose the hidden list. You can also use “Show or Hide All Notes.” Collapsing is different from hiding: double-click empty header space to roll a note down again.

## A shortcut does not work

Open Settings → Shortcuts. Another application may already use the global shortcut. Change the binding or retry registration using the warning icon. MakeTask does not require Accessibility permission to register global shortcuts.

## Saving warning

Keep MakeTask open, check available disk space and access to the local app data, and use “Retry Saving.” The warning remains until a save succeeds. Avoid quitting while changes are unsaved.

## Back up or transfer lists

Use Settings → Backup → Export, then import the JSON file on the target Mac. Imports add lists without overwriting existing ones. Exported files contain readable task data and are not encrypted by MakeTask; store them somewhere appropriate for their contents.

## Due dates and reminders

Due dates and overdue warnings appear inside your notes. MakeTask 1.0 does not send scheduled reminder notifications and does not synchronize between Macs.

## Remove data

Delete tasks or lists inside MakeTask. Delete exported backups and any copies separately in Finder or your storage provider. Uninstalling the app alone may leave its local sandbox data and exported backups on your Mac.

## Privacy

[Read the privacy policy](../MakeTask/Resources/PrivacyPolicy.md). A bundled copy is also available offline in Settings → About → Privacy Policy.
