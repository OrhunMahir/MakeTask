MakeTask 1.1.1 adds a persistent Dock icon while the app is running. No account or demo credentials are required.

To test: launch MakeTask and create a list. Add another list using Command-Shift-N. Hide the notes with Note > Show or Hide All Notes, then click MakeTask in the Dock. All notes reappear. Clicking the Dock icon again keeps them visible, preserving note positions and collapsed layouts.

Command-Shift-H continues to toggle existing notes; with no lists it does nothing. Command-Shift-Space opens Quick Add. The menu bar and Notification Center widgets remain available.

When no lists exist, an explicit app launch or Dock click shows the welcome screen on first use, or New List after onboarding. Login-item and service launches do not reveal hidden notes. Widget links continue to open the selected list or task.

Task data remains local in the existing app container. No analytics, tracking, accounts, servers or cloud sync are used. Settings > About contains the offline privacy policy.
