MakeTask 1.1.0 adds a macOS WidgetKit extension. The app requires macOS 14 or later.

MakeTask is a menu-bar app with independent desktop notes. No account, subscription, server, or demo credentials are required. Existing local tasks remain in the same app database. The widget reads a local snapshot in an App Group; completion actions execute in the main app and update that database.

To review the widget:
1. Launch MakeTask, create a list, and add a few tasks.
2. Open Notification Center by clicking the date/time, choose Edit Widgets, search for MakeTask, and add a small, medium, or large widget.
3. Edit the widget to select a list and optionally show completed tasks.
4. Click a task's circle to complete or uncomplete it. Repeat with MakeTask quit to test background launch.
5. Click New task to open Quick Add with that list selected, enter a title, and press Return.
6. Click a task title to open its details in MakeTask, or the widget's list title to open the full note.

Text entry and editing open the corresponding app window; they do not take place inside WidgetKit. Widget refresh timing is controlled by macOS.

The app collects no data and has no analytics or tracking. The privacy policy is available offline in Settings > About. Notifications are not required for widget use.
