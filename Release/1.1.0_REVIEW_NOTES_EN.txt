MakeTask 1.1.0 adds Notification Center widgets on macOS 14 or later. No account or demo credentials are required.

To test: launch MakeTask, create a list and add tasks. Open Notification Center > Edit Widgets, search for MakeTask and add a widget. Edit it to choose a list and optionally show completed tasks. Click a task circle to complete or uncomplete it, including after quitting MakeTask. Click New task to open Quick Add for that list. Click a task title to open its details.

Text entry opens the app window. Widgets read a local shared snapshot; completion updates the existing local app database. No analytics, tracking, servers or cloud sync are used. Existing task data remains in the same app container.

MakeTask lives in the menu bar without a permanent Dock icon. The welcome window lets new users create their first list. Settings > About contains the offline privacy policy.
